Naming — the machine and the system are different words

Tritium

hardware

The physical server: its hostname and network identity. The name is fixed. Change it and certificates, host lookups and config values break.

Quantum

system

The AI infrastructure that runs on Tritium, renamed away from the clashing name. Never find-and-replace one word for the other.
