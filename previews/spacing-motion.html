Space, Elevation & Motion

Theme-invariant tokens: they don't change between dark and light, so tokens.css declares them once, in their own :root block. Spacing sits on a 4px grid. Motion is short, eased and always degradable.

Motion — press play; reduced motion collapses all of it
linear · reference
--ease-panel · --dur-panel .35s
--ease-bounce · --dur-enter .3s

Hover, focus and colour changes are always --dur-micro (.15s). A spinner under reduced motion does not freeze. A frozen spinner reads as "hung", so it swaps rotation for an opacity breath.

Touch floor — --tap 44px (46px on coarse pointers)
Beta env: production

A control can be drawn small. Its hit area can't be: a transparent ::before pads the badge ×, chip ×, sort header and checkbox out to 44px without changing what you see.
