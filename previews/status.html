State & Records

A state indicator is a status-plane projection: it must keep rendering when the agent or node it describes is down, so stopped and unknown are states it reports, not failures it suffers. Each state has its own shape and, when live, its own motion. Switch on reduced motion or grayscale and every state is still distinguishable.

Records — goals and ADRs with a lifecycle

An ID, a codename, a title and a status in words. The lifecycle is Draft · Active · Pending Review · Completed · Archived; a superseded record keeps its row and points at its successor rather than being edited away.
