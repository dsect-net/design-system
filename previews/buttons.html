Buttons

Every interactive control enforces a 44px touch-target floor (46px under coarse-pointer media), regardless of visible size. Radius is always --radius — buttons never borrow the larger card radius. There is no dedicated danger button; destructive actions are carried by --red text/icon color on a standard variant, not a separate shape: add .is-destructive.
