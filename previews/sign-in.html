Sign-in

The one auth screen for every app, canonised from the Nebula login. A centred 400px card: the app's mark, name and subtitle, a segmented Email | Passphrase switch, provider buttons under an “or” rule, and two quiet footer lines. Apps supply their own mark, name, footer copy and submit wiring; the shape, spacing and states live in components.css (.sign-in) and in @dsect/ui as <SignIn>. All copy on this page is sample copy.

Error — field and form

A rejected sign-in marks the credential fields (aria-invalid: red border plus an icon, so colour is never the only signal) and adds one plain-language line as role="alert". OAuth failures land in the same alert line.
