App Shell

The hub as an installed app on a phone. A compact app bar, a bottom tab bar in thumb reach, bottom sheets instead of centred modals, and the safe areas a notch and home indicator take. Framed at the two widths the hub's touch test runs at. Open the shell on its own to try it at your own width.

What the layer does

  • .app is a 100dvh column; the tab bar is sticky at its foot, so content never scrolls under it and no page needs bottom padding hacks.
  • .tabbar renders only below 760px, the width at which the sidenav stops. A page uses it or the hamburger drawer. One mobile nav.
  • Every tab is 58px tall. The active tab takes the system's one "you are here" mark, a 2px emerald bar, and aria-current="page".
  • .sheet is the phone's modal: a native <dialog> on the bottom edge, capped at 85% height, padded clear of the home indicator.
  • viewport-fit=cover + env(safe-area-inset-*) keep the app bar and tab bar out of the notch and home indicator.
  • Installed (display-mode: standalone): no rubber-band overscroll, .app-only shows, .browser-only hides.
  • touch-action: manipulation removes the double-tap-zoom delay; the grey tap flash is replaced by the system's own :active states.
  • Toasts on phones already sit 68px up, clear of the 58px tab bar.
